# DDD Original VFX Sound Effects

These sound effects are original procedural assets generated for this project.
They do not contain purchased audio, downloaded samples, recordings, or third-party sound libraries.

- Format: 48,000 Hz / stereo / 192 kbps MP3
- Assets: 352 original files across every DDD tribe plus Obelisk, Osiris, and Ra
- Phases: charge, launch, impact, aftermath
- Levels: Lv1 is compact, Lv2 establishes the core identity, Lv3 adds signature accents,
  and Lv4 adds a second impact, deeper body, wider reflections, and a longer tail
- Divine beasts: intentionally fixed-level; Obelisk, Osiris, and Ra use individual authored kits
- Generator: `scripts/generate-ddd-vfx-se.py`
- Intended use: DDD battle presentation and the developer FX LAB preview

The generator layers filtered noise, physical resonances, modal impacts, turbulent air,
granular debris, and synthetic room responses. Vocal and creature layers use a source-filter
model with irregular glottal pulses, pitch jitter, vocal fry, breath, throat turbulence, chest
resonance, and early room reflections. No recorded impulse responses are used.

Charge cues do not share a common riser. Their leading identities are authored separately:

- Undead: a breathy descending death wail, cold air, and death-rattle details in a distant room
- Angel: a detuned multi-voice church choir and long bell modes in a cathedral-scale space
- Psychic: unstable magnetic pressure, heartbeat-like low modes, and dry electrical ticks
- Machine: motor spin-up followed by timed ratchets and a final hydraulic valve release
- Cyberse: short digital handshake tones, gated data texture, and clean electrical arcs
- Dragon: inhale, asymmetric laryngeal roar, chest resonance, and hot turbulent breath
- Dinosaur: a lower chest bellow with irregular throat pulses and ground-weighted resonance
- Beast: close snarl, panting mouth noise, chest growl, and a claw-positioning scrape
- Demon: breath consonants and organic "hi-hi-hi" cackle syllables over a low demonic double
- Warrior: leather grip, sword-draw friction, blade resonance, and a ready-position clang
- Insect: two non-identical continuous wing pairs with chitin ticks and close dry reflections
- Plant: individually timed wet/dry leaf passes, fiber movement, and a bending stem resonance
- Aquatic: non-periodic water pressure, differently sized bubbles, and low underwater movement
- Fire: multiscale flame turbulence, low combustion body, and irregular high crackles
- Winged beast: discrete downstrokes with low wing-body pressure and separated feather passes
- Rock: irregular stone-on-stone grind, loose gravel, and a low structural groan
- Spellcaster: breathy spoken chant articulation and resonant rune modes
- Illusion: displaced whispers, reverse breath, and glass-like resonances
- Wyrm: a lighter serpent call with forked hiss articulation
